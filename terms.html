Commission Guidelines & Workflow

A simple overview of how I handle projects and development

Development Process

1

Initial Discussion

Reach out via Discord or Roblox DM with your game concept, required features, and general timeline so we can look over the details.

2

Project Summary

I will send over a clear breakdown listing the specific mechanics we discussed along with the overall pricing for the work.

3

Milestone Structure (For Larger Projects)

For larger systems, the project is divided into clear stages to make tracking progress and testing simple:

Working inside your group environment

• A 20% deposit secures the schedule so I can begin scripting.

• Milestone payments are cleared as you check and confirm the progress.

• Work moves forward step-by-step to keep the codebase organized.

Working inside my test environment

• A 20% deposit opens the project in a separate test place.

• You have playtest access to try out the new systems whenever you want.

• To keep development moving smoothly, I can script up to 30% ahead while you test.

4

Service Formats

Depending on what your project needs, we can use either workflow option:

Per-Task Arrangement

• Best for standalone mechanics, quick feature updates, or specific fixes.

• We confirm the exact task, clear the payment, and I deliver the code.

• Let’s you go down your feature checklist at your own pace.

Hourly Sprints

• Best for general live development, brainstorming, or tweaking complex systems.

• Paid in advance for a set block of hours (like a focused 3-hour session) to secure my time.

• Can easily be renewed daily as we establish a steady project workflow.

5

Final Handover

Once everything is fully built, tested, and approved, the remaining balance is cleared, and all optimized scripts are delivered directly to you.

Accepted Channels

  • Payoneer: Preferred for milestone setups and larger project contracts.
  • Robux: Settled through direct Group Payouts or Gamepasses (with the standard 30% Roblox platform tax covered).
  • Alternative Options: Open to other common corporate payment tools depending on what your team uses.

General Guidelines

  • Custom quotes are calculated based on USD value to keep pricing stable.
  • To keep accounting straightforward, standard transaction fees are covered on the client's side.
  • Security Note: To keep things safe and avoid platform issues, I do not use PayPal, Crypto, or Gift Cards.
  • Commitment: Because deposits lock in dedicated development time for your system architecture, payments are final once work on a block starts.

Terms & Licensing

Usage & Implementation Rights

  • I retain the rights to my foundational code libraries so I can continue using my personal tools.
  • Commercial Use: After the final balance is cleared, you have full rights to use the scripts across ANY project or group under your brand.
  • Distribution: The code is for your games only—please do not resell or distribute the standalone scripts on public marketplaces.
  • Portfolio: I reserve the right to show short video clips or playtest demonstrations of the work in my portfolio.

Workflow Flow

  • We follow the agreed milestone order to ensure the code stays clean and bug-free.
  • Sticking to the planned steps keeps the project moving along on schedule.

Included Support

  • Bug Fixes: Includes up to 3 rounds of minor fixes to make sure everything runs smoothly during launch.
  • If you want to add entirely new features outside the initial summary later on, we can easily set up a fresh quote for them.
